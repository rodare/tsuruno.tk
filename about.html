About

Tsuruno comes from the japanese words "tsuru" and "no" literally meaning "from the crane" in English, as a reference to the japanese tale "鶴の恩返し" ("tsuru no ongaeshi") also translated in English as "The grateful crane". The reason for this naming is that the purpose of this community site is to enhance and organize initiatives by its members based on the feeling of reciprocity. By sharing what each of us can bring to this community, we build stronger tights between each other and enjoy the time spent together. Nothing but the desire to reciprocate keeps this group going.
